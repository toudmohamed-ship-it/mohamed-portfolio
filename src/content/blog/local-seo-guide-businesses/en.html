Local SEO is the work that gets a business shown when people search for a service in a place: in the map results, in Google Maps and in the regular results below them. This guide covers how Google ranks local results, how to set up a Google Business Profile within Google's rules, which keywords and pages to build, how to get reviews, and a 90-day plan for a law firm, a clinic or any business that serves clients in a defined area.

What is local SEO?

Local SEO is search engine optimization for searches with a place in them, written ("family lawyer Casablanca") or implied ("dentist near me", where Google uses the searcher's location). It has two parts: the Google Business Profile, which appears in the map results, and the website, which supports the profile and ranks in the regular results.

These searches come from people who need the service now and nearby, so a business that appears in them gets calls, direction requests and form enquiries rather than readers.

How does Google rank local results?

Google says local results are mainly based on three things: relevance, distance and prominence (Google Business Profile Help).

How to set up your Google Business Profile

Set up the profile with your real business details, the fewest categories that describe your core business, and complete information, because Google says complete and accurate profiles are more likely to show in local results. Google's guidelines for representing your business set the rules:

  • Claim and verify the profile. Only a verified owner can edit it and reply to reviews.
  • Use your real business name. The name on your sign, website and stationery, with no service or city added. Extra words in the name are against the guidelines and can get the profile suspended.
  • Choose few, specific categories. Google asks for the fewest categories that describe your core business, as specific as possible, and not categories used as keywords.
  • Address or service area. A business that visits clients and does not receive them should hide its address and set a service area.
  • Complete the rest. Hours (including holiday hours), phone number, website, services and attributes.
  • Add real photos. Your team, your office and your work. Photos show clients what you offer.

If a profile is suspended, do not create a new one: follow Google's reinstatement process. A law firm's suspended profile was recovered this way during a local SEO project.

Which local keywords to target

Target searches that combine a service with a place, such as [service] + [city], plus the questions clients ask before they hire. Three kinds matter:

  • Service and place: "family lawyer Casablanca", "emergency plumber Marrakech".
  • Near me: "dentist near me". Google fills in the place from the searcher's location, so the page needs the service and the area you cover, not the words "near me".
  • Questions: "how much does a divorce lawyer cost in Rabat". These suit articles that link to the service page.

Low-volume searches with a clear intent to hire are often worth more than broad ones. The keyword research process shows how to find them with Search Console and keyword tools.

How to build a page for each city you serve

Build one page for each city where you really serve clients, and write each one for that city. A page that copies another and swaps the city name gives Google little reason to index it.

  • Title and H1: the service and the city, for example "Family Lawyer in Casablanca | Firm Name".
  • Local details: the address or the areas covered, how to get there, and work done in that city.
  • Proof: reviews and results from clients in that area, if you have them.
  • Contact: the phone number and a short form near the top, and a map on the contact page.

What citations are and why consistency matters

Citations are mentions of your business name, address and phone number (NAP) on other websites, such as directories, industry listings and local news sites. Keep the details identical everywhere, so Google can match every mention to the same business.

  • Start with the main directories in your country and in your industry.
  • Fix old details after a move or a phone change. Tools such as BrightLocal list where they still appear.
  • Earn local links. Google counts links to your website as part of prominence: local associations, partners, events and press.

How to get more Google reviews within Google's rules

Ask every client for an honest review, make it easy, and reply to each one. Google says more reviews and positive ratings can help local ranking. Its review policy also sets limits:

Make the request part of your normal follow-up, for example the message that closes a case or confirms an appointment, so it reaches every client and not a chosen few.

What technical SEO a local website needs

A local website needs fast mobile pages, city and service pages that Google can index, and business details in structured data that match the profile.

  • Mobile speed: most local searches happen on a phone. Check Core Web Vitals in PageSpeed Insights and Search Console.
  • Indexable pages: confirm in Search Console that every service and city page is indexed.
  • LocalBusiness schema: JSON-LD with the same name, address, phone and hours as the profile.
  • Hreflang: if the site has several languages, link each page to its translations.

A 90-day local SEO plan

Spend the first month on the profile and the errors, the second on pages, and the third on reviews and citations:

  • Days 1 to 30: claim and complete the Google Business Profile, list where your NAP appears and fix differences, fix indexing and speed problems.
  • Days 31 to 60: build or rewrite the service pages and one page per city you serve.
  • Days 61 to 90: start asking every client for a review, list the business in the main directories, and look for local links.

Then measure calls, direction requests and form enquiries each month in the profile's performance report and in GA4, and repeat what works.
